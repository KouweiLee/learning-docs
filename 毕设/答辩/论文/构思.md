Virtio的优势

Virtio is very popular for virtual I/O devices because it provides a straightforward, efficient, standard, and extensible mechanism, and eliminates the need for boutique, per-environment, or per-OS mechanisms. For example, rather than having a variety of device emulation mechanisms, virtio provides a common frontend driver framework that standardizes device interfaces, and increases code reuse across different virtualization platforms.

## 摘要

虚拟化技术已广泛应用于嵌入式领域。虚拟机监控器允许在一台物理主机上创建并运行多个虚拟机，并提供虚拟设备供其使用。其中，Virtio半虚拟设备以其高性能、低延迟、可复用的优点，已被广泛实现和应用。

本文基于一个ARMv8体系结构下的虚拟机监控器hvsior，设计并实现了一种新型的Virtio设备后端，包含Virtio磁盘设备和网络设备。受hvisor轻量级特性的限制，本文将Virtio设备实现为管理虚拟机上的一个守护进程，由该进程解析驱动发起的IO操作，并通过管理虚拟机上的原生驱动操作真实设备，完成IO操作。为了提高其他虚拟机上的Virtio驱动和设备的通信效率，降低通信延迟，本文在守护进程和Hypervisor之间实现了一个基于共享内存的通信跳板，该跳板负责传递驱动和设备的控制面交互信息。此外，本文还针对Virtio设备实现了多线程优化以及休眠机制，进一步提升IO性能，并显著降低CPU功耗。

经过测试，本文实现的Virtio磁盘性能最高可达真实设备的70%，Virtio网卡性能接近真实网卡的网络速度，同时仅为hvisor引入了320行代码。因此，本文实现的Virtio设备，不仅保持了虚拟机监控器的轻量性，也保证了设备良好的性能表现。



本文尝试为虚拟机监控器hvisor探索一种新型的Virtio设备实现机制，保证虚拟机监控器的轻量性，同时兼顾设备性能。

## 创新点

本文实现了一种新型的Virtio后端机制。Virtio后端设备实现在了管理虚拟机的用户态守护进程中，其他虚拟机上的Virtio驱动与设备通信通过通信跳板。通信跳板是守护进程与Hypervisor之间的一片共享内存区域，用于传递驱动和设备之间控制面交互命令和结果。

Virtualization technology has been widely applied in the embedded field. A hypervisor allows the creation and operation of multiple virtual machines on a single physical host and provides virtual devices for their use. Among these, Virtio semi-virtual devices are widely implemented and utilized due to their high performance, low latency, and reusability.
This paper designs and implements a new Virtio device backend for a hypervisor based on the ARMv8 architecture, named hvsior, which includes Virtio block and network devices. Due to the lightweight nature of hvsior, Virtio devices are implemented as a daemon process on the management virtual machine. This process interprets I/O operations initiated by drivers and carries out these operations with real devices through the native drivers on the management virtual machine. To improve the communication efficiency and reduce the latency between Virtio devices and drivers, a shared memory-based communication bridge is established between the daemon process and hypervisor. The bridge facilitates the interactions between drivers and devices. Additionally, the paper implements multi-threading optimization and a sleep mechanism for Virtio devices to enhance I/O performance and reduce CPU consumption.
Tests have shown that the performance of the Virtio disk can reach up to 70% of real devices, and the Virtio network card's performance is close to the real network card, adding only 320 lines of code to hvsior. Therefore, the Virtio devices not only maintain the lightweight nature of the hypervisor but also ensure good performance of the devices.
